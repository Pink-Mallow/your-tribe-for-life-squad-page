# Refinement
Refinement are rules regarding how a task can be set up in the backlog before it can be moved to "Ready" to be picked up.

Refine ideas, tasks, and vague needs in the backlog into work that can be picked up in any sprint.

Good to remember:
- DON'T LET IT EAT DUST!     
  A task may be suitable for a certain period, but principles, conventions, rules, WCAG requirements, and the market can change. 
  So, keep your tasks up to date—check them regularly!
- Refinement ensures you have a clear overview of your tasks- which ones you can start working on (depending on your sprint goal and planning) and whether or not they are clearly defined.
